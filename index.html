Rules & T&Cs
Read it. Yes, all of it.

Welcome to RACE DAY

Welcome to RACE DAY — where your knowledge of horse racing will be thoroughly tested, brutally exposed, and occasionally rewarded. Pick your horses, back your judgment, and accept that sometimes a $81 roughie will ruin your entire afternoon. We're all in this together. Mostly.

How It Works

Each week admin selects a card of races. Pick one horse per race, hope for the best, and talk plenty of trash in the chat when your horse wins.

You get an imaginary $1 bet per race. Payouts are based on official Sportsbet win and place dividends:

  • 1st place — collects win dividend + place dividend
  • 2nd or 3rd place — collects place dividend only
  • Unplaced — collects nothing, stares at the wall

Your total across all races is your score. Highest score wins.

Entry Fee

Entry is $10 per valid entry. Cash goes in the plastic sleeve in the office — not to admin, not through this app, directly into the sleeve.

Once the winner is declared, they'll post their bank details and everyone transfers directly. Multiple completed entries means multiple $10 entry fees. Pay up.

This app does not handle, process, or track any money. Entry fees and payments are entirely between participants.

Scratchings

If your horse gets scratched, you'll automatically be allocated the pre-race favourite, captured at race time. What happens to that favourite after is between you and the racing gods.

Valid Entries

  • You must select a horse for every race to be a valid participant. Partial entries are null and void — no exceptions.
  • Multiple completed entries under the same name count as separate participants, each requiring a $10 entry fee.
  • Registered but forgot to pick? Null and void. Set a reminder next time champ.

Selections

  • All selections must be locked in before the registration cutoff time
  • Once the cutoff hits, selections are frozen — no changes, no swaps
  • Races are published by Thursday so you have no excuse

Results

Results will be updated by admin as soon as possible after each race settles. Refresh regularly, keep an eye on the chat, and try not to message admin every five minutes asking why it hasn't been updated. It'll get done.

General

  • This is for fun. If you're not having fun, that's on you.
  • Admin's decision is final. Yes, final. Don't @ us.
  • RACE DAY takes no responsibility for horses that "definitely had it won" until they didn't.
